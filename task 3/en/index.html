Dam and Pine Forest Escape

Theme: Peaceful Water, Woodland trails, Silent Village


Stops

  • Geçitköy Reservoir
  • Pine Forest Trailhead
  • Geçitköy Villiage (Stone houses)
  • Picnic area
  • Panoramic dam Viewpoint

Total Circuit Distance: ~45 km
Estimated Duration: 3–4 hours
Estimated Budget: ~1900 TL per person

insider tip
Get there a little bit after sunrise when the water is like glass. Additionally the singing of the birds echos across the reservoir magically. You will have the enire forest to yourself.

Beylerbeyi Artisans & Olive Groves Loop

Theme: Labouring artists, pottery, olive groves and a lengthy village lunch


Stops
  • Beylerbeyi Village Square
  • A working pottery studio
  • Geçitköy Villiage (Stone houses)
  • Picnic area
  • Panoramic dam Viewpoint

Total Circuit Distance: ~55 km
Estimated Duration: 3.5–4.5 hours
Estimated Budget: ~2000 TL per person

insider tip
Arrive in late afternoon. The light on the old stone buildings is perfect for photos, and the parking is much easier once the tour buses leave

Kalkanlı monumental olive trees & METU campus route

Theme: Nature & Eco - Photograpgy


Stops
  • The monumental olive grove outside Kalkanlı village
  • Choban Cave nearby
  • a viewpoint over the protected Kalkanlı valley
  • the university campus grounds
  • a rest stop at the marked memorial trees

Total Circuit Distance: ~90 km
Estimated Duration: 4–5 hour
Estimated Budget: ~1,200 TL per person

insider tip
come on the last Sunday of March, when the annual Monumental Olive Trees Walk draws locals — regularly it's usually just you and the birds. Also worth knowing: a 2020 wildfire destroyed about 90% of the roughly 2,000 trees here, so the surviving giants are treated as protected monuments — stay on marked paths.

Bronze Age Cyprus route

Theme: deep archaeologys


Stops
  • Enkomi ancient city
  • Famagusta District countryside road
  • a scenic stop at the site's edge overlooking the old riverbed
  • a local café in Tuzla villages
  • Gazimağusa city walls approach

Total Circuit Distance: ~140 km
Estimated Duration: 5–6 hours
Estimated Budget: ~2200 TL per perso

insider tip
The site sits several kilometers inland currently — the river that once made it a working harbour silted up centuries ago, so bring water and expect an unshaded walk with no facilities on-site.

Karaoğlanoğlu memorial & west coast

Theme: deep archaeologys


Stops
  • the Şehitlik memorial garden
  • the Peace and Freedom Museum
  • a coastal viewpoint just past Karaoğlanoğlu
  • the Alsancak shoreline
  • a small local café to close the route

Total Circuit Distance: ~20 km
Estimated Duration: 2–3 hours
Estimated Budget: ~1800 TL per person

insider tip
The museum tells the story of the 1974 conflict from one side, not in a neutral way. Good to know before you go.
